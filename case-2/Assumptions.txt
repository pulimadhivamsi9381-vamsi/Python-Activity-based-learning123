ASSUMPTIONS

1. There are exactly five subjects.
2. Each subject is marked out of 100.
3. Pass mark is 35 in each subject.
4. Grace marks are allowed in exactly one failed subject only when its mark is between 30 and 34 inclusive.
5. Only enough marks to reach 35 are added; the adjusted mark cannot exceed 35 under this rule.
6. Grace marks are an academic rule assumed for this case study, not an official institution policy.
