OBJECTIVES

1. Accept electricity units consumed from the user.
2. Validate that the entered units are not negative.
3. Calculate energy charges using progressive slabs.
4. Add the fixed charge.
5. Apply the surcharge or rebate according to the assumptions.
6. Display the bill details and final amount.
7. Practice variables, input/output, type conversion, arithmetic operators,
   relational operators, and if-elif-else decision statements.
