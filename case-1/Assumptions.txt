ASSUMPTIONS

The assignment topic does not provide exact tariff values. The following
values are assumed only for this case study:

1. First 100 units: Rs. 1.50 per unit.
2. Units 101 to 200: Rs. 2.50 per unit.
3. Units 201 to 400: Rs. 4.00 per unit.
4. Units above 400: Rs. 6.00 per unit.
5. Fixed charge: Rs. 50.00 per bill.
6. If the energy charge is greater than Rs. 1,000, add a 5% surcharge
   on the energy charge.
7. If consumption is 100 units or less, give a 2% rebate on the energy charge.
8. The surcharge and rebate are mutually exclusive because their conditions
   cannot both be true.
9. Negative unit values are invalid.
10. Currency is displayed in Indian rupees (Rs.).
