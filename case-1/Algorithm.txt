ALGORITHM

1. Start.
2. Read the number of units consumed.
3. If units are negative, display an invalid-input message and stop.
4. Set energy_charge to zero.
5. If units are at most 100, multiply all units by Rs. 1.50.
6. Otherwise, charge Rs. 1.50 for the first 100 units.
7. If units are above 100, charge up to the next 100 units at Rs. 2.50.
8. If units are above 200, charge up to the next 200 units at Rs. 4.00.
9. If units are above 400, charge the remaining units at Rs. 6.00.
10. Add the fixed charge of Rs. 50.
11. If energy charge is greater than Rs. 1,000, calculate a 5% surcharge.
12. Else, if units are at most 100, calculate a 2% rebate.
13. Calculate final bill = energy charge + fixed charge + surcharge - rebate.
14. Display the bill details.
15. Stop.
