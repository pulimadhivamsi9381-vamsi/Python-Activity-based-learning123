ASSUMPTIONS

1. The user enters records for 3 days.
2. Quantities are measured in kilograms and may be decimal values.
3. Prepared quantity must be greater than zero for each day.
4. Wasted quantity must be between zero and prepared quantity.
5. Overall waste below 5% is LOW; 5% through 10% is MODERATE; above 10% is HIGH.
6. These thresholds are academic assumptions for demonstration, not official standards.
