ALGORITHM

1. Read conducted and attended class counts.
2. Validate that both are non-negative and attended is not greater than conducted.
3. Calculate current attendance percentage (attended/conducted) × 100.
4. If current attendance is at least 75%, display that no recovery is needed.
5. Otherwise, start additional_classes at zero and repeat: increase additional_classes until attended + additional_classes is at least 75% of conducted + additional_classes.
6. Display current percentage and required additional classes.
7. Stop.
